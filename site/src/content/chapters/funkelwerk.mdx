The stars behind everything else. A catalog of the roughly nine thousand stars visible to the naked eye, each with its
position, brightness and color, turned into the observer's sky by the sidereal time from [sternzeit](../sternzeit/).
How many of them show, and how much they twinkle, depends on the atmosphere of [dunstkreis](../dunstkreis/) in front of
them and on the Moon's light from [nachtgestirn](../nachtgestirn/).
