Clouds, the part of the sky that changes by the minute. They are lit by the Sun and the Moon, whose directions come
from [sternzeit](../sternzeit/), take their colors from the light that [dunstkreis](../dunstkreis/) lets through the
atmosphere, and hide the stars of [sternenzelt](../sternenzelt/) and the Moon of [nachtgestirn](../nachtgestirn/)
behind them.
